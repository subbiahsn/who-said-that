    How to play

    1

    Read the fact. Each door shows one surprising fact about someone in the room.

    2

    Type a name. Start typing and a dropdown of names appears. Pick the one you think said it.

    3

    Lock it in. Press Lock in my guess to actually submit. Nothing counts until you lock it, and it can't be changed.

    4

    Pick your favourites. Tap the ♥ Favourite fact button to mark a fact you love. Tap again to unmark it.
